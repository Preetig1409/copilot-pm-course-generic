Action Plan

Three phases to take us from validated findings to a deployed, field-ready course.

  1. 1

    Implement Fixes & Improvements

    Address all 130 actionable findings. Critical items (keyboard shortcuts, enterprise sensitivity) first, then major, then moderate. Each fix will reference the finding ID (e.g., T1-02) for traceability.

  2. 2

    Validation Round

    Share the updated course back with all 4 teams. Each team verifies their findings are addressed: bugs fixed, suggestions incorporated, or rationale provided for any deferred items. Lightweight pass — not a full re-test.

  3. 3

    Course Launch & Field Deployment

    Deploy the compliant course to the field. Testers become champions who train Scrum Masters, who then train Product Owners. Value is realized only when people in the field are learning.

We have created value through building this course. But we have not yet realized value. Value realization happens only when the course is deployed in the field and practitioners are learning from it. That is our north star.
