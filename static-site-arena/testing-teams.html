Role Definitions

Tester (all 15)

  • Completes all 15 modules end-to-end
  • Applies common lenses (L1, L2) with full attention to every module
  • Applies owned lenses (2 per team) with deep focus to every module
  • Notes obvious issues on other teams' lenses without deep investigation
  • Submits structured feedback using the template

Team Lead (4)

  • All tester responsibilities above
  • Reviews all feedback from their team for quality, accuracy, and relevance
  • Returns vague, duplicate, or unsubstantiated feedback to the tester
  • Forwards validated feedback to the cross-team lead for second approval
  • Participates in the Meta-Team

Meta-Team (4 leads + course author)

  • Meets twice: mid-sprint sync and end-of-sprint review
  • Deduplicates findings across teams (4 teams testing the same modules will find overlapping issues)
  • Resolves disagreements between leads
  • Prioritizes the consolidated report by severity and theme
  • Delivers final report to course author

Testing Phases

Phase 1: Foundation Pass (Days 1-4)

All testers work through Level 1 modules (1.1-1.10). This is the largest level and establishes baseline experience.

1Kickoff (60 min): Strategy walkthrough, teams confirmed, practice feedback submission

4Team leads begin reviewing incoming feedback

Phase 2: Advanced & Visuals Pass (Days 5-8)

All testers complete Level 2 (2.1-2.3) and Level 3 (3.1-3.2).

5-6Modules 2.1-2.3 (advanced PM workflows)

7Mid-sprint sync (Meta-Team, 30 min): Cross-team patterns, emerging themes, deduplication preview
8Catch-up day: complete any remaining modules, finalize feedback submissions

Phase 3: Validation & Consolidation (Days 9-14)

DayActivity

12Meta-Team working session: deduplicate cross-team findings, resolve challenges
13Meta-Team finalizes consolidated report
14Final report delivered to course author
