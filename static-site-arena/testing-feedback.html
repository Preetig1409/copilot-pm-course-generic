Feedback & Quality Gates

Every piece of feedback passes through two validation checkpoints before reaching the course author.

Feedback Quality Gate

Checkpoint 1: Team Lead Review

The team lead evaluates each feedback item against these criteria:

CriteriaPassFail (return to tester)
SpecificReferences exact module, section, or line"The course is confusing"
Observation + ExpectationBoth fields filled with concrete detailVague or missing either field
Non-duplicateNot already submitted by another team memberRepeat of existing finding
Right lensesTagged lenses actually match the findingPrompt issue tagged as UX only

Lead actions:

  • Approve — Forward to cross-team validation
  • Return — Send back with note on what to clarify (tester revises and resubmits)

Checkpoint 2: Cross-Team Lead Validation

A lead from a different team reviews approved feedback:

They check forWhy
AccuracyDoes the finding hold up? Is the "bug" actually a bug?
RelevanceIs this about the course, or about Copilot's general limitations?
ProportionalityDoes the severity match the impact?
Cross-team contextDid another team already find the same thing? Does a different lens add nuance?

Feedback Template

One item per finding. Keep it simple — say what you saw, what you expected, and which lenses it touches.

## [Module ID] — [Short title]

**Observation:** [What you saw or experienced — be specific, quote text or describe the step]

**Expectation:** [What should have happened instead]

**Lenses:** [L1, L3] ← tag 1–3 lenses this relates to

That's it. No severity ratings, no type checkboxes, no evidence sections. The course author will triage priority. Your job is to notice and describe — clearly and specifically.

Example

## 1.3 — Prompt output doesn't match screenshot

**Observation:** The prompt "Summarize these meeting notes into action items" on page 3
returns a bulleted list, but the module shows a numbered table with owners and due dates.

**Expectation:** Output should match or closely resemble the screenshot shown in the module.

**Lenses:** [L3, L2]

Consolidated Report Format

The Meta-Team delivers one final report to the course author.

7.1 Executive Summary

  • Total findings: submitted → approved → endorsed (funnel)
  • Breakdown by lens (which lenses surfaced the most issues?)
  • Top 3 cross-cutting themes

7.2 Findings by Module

All endorsed findings grouped by module (1.1 through 3.2), with lens tags.

7.3 Findings by Lens

Same findings regrouped by lens — reveals systemic patterns.

7.4 Cross-Team Agreement

Findings where multiple teams independently flagged the same issue — high-confidence problems.

7.5 Positive Findings

What works well, organized by lens. Protect these during revisions.
