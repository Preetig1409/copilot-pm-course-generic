Stakeholder Interview #5: Project Manager Persona

Participant: Kevin Park Role: Project Manager, Cloud & Infrastructure Location: Seattle, WA Tenure: 7 years at NovaTech Solutions Current Project: Multi-cloud implementation for insurance client Products Used: ProjectPulse (daily), KnowledgeHub (weekly) Date: February 11, 2025 Interviewer: Senior Product Owner Duration: 35 minutes

Background

Kevin manages a large cloud implementation project with a team of 35 team members. He's been at NovaTech Solutions for 7 years and has managed projects ranging from $5M to $50M in value. He's meticulous about project tracking but frustrated by the tools available.

Interview

Q: How does ProjectPulse fit into your daily work?

Kevin: "Every day I'm supposed to update project status in ProjectPulse. Budget burn rate, milestone progress, risks, team capacity. In theory it's supposed to take 15 minutes. In practice, it takes me an hour because the interface is clunky, data entry is painful, and I'm duplicating information I've already captured elsewhere."

Q: Tell me about the status reporting challenge.

Kevin: "I have multiple stakeholders who want status updates. The client wants a weekly status call with slides. The program manager wants a different format. The department lead wants high-level metrics. Finance wants project financial updates. I'm basically creating the same content four different ways. ProjectPulse should be generating these reports for me, but it doesn't."

Q: What about the project health indicators in ProjectPulse?

Kevin: "They're useless. The system shows 'green' if I mark it green. It doesn't actually assess project health. If I'm burning budget 20% faster than planned, the system doesn't know or care. I have to manually calculate that and update the status. What I want is: ProjectPulse looks at hours logged, budget consumed, milestones completed, and tells me 'Hey, you're trending toward a 15% overrun.' Automatically."

Q: How do you handle resource requests?

Kevin: "Right now it's email and spreadsheets. I need a data engineer starting April 1. I submit a request, it goes into a queue, someone manually reviews it, looks for available people. Takes days, sometimes weeks. By the time I get someone, my timeline has slipped. I've started requesting resources earlier than I need them, just to buffer the slow process."

Q: What about when you need to find experts for a specific technical challenge?

Kevin: "That's where KnowledgeHub comes in, theoretically. I search for 'Azure Kubernetes migration' experts, I get a list. But I don't know who's good. I usually end up asking my program manager or posting in Teams channels. The expert search in KnowledgeHub is a starting point but not the answer."

Q: Tell me about team capacity tracking.

Kevin: "My team is supposed to log time in ProjectPulse. Some do it daily, some do it weekly, some forget until someone reminds them. The mobile time entry is terrible, so people avoid it. The data ends up being 2-3 weeks stale. When Elena asks me about team capacity, I have to chase people down to update their timesheets before I can answer accurately."

Q: What would make your job easier?

Kevin: "Three things. First, automated status reports - pull data from the system and generate a report I can customize. Second, real health indicators that actually look at data and tell me when something's wrong. Third, faster resource allocation - I should be able to see who's available and request them in minutes, not days."

Q: How does this compare to tools at other firms?

Kevin: "I hear mixed things. Friends at Notion complain too, but they say things are improving. Monday.com apparently has better tools, at least that's what team members who've worked there tell me. Atlassian is supposed to be gold standard but they're smaller, so maybe it's just easier. At our scale, everything is harder."

Q: Any final thoughts?

Kevin: "The fundamental problem is that ProjectPulse feels like it was designed for finance and HR, not for project managers. The interface, the workflows, the reports - they're not oriented toward how I actually manage a project. It's like someone built a system and then said 'PMs can use it too.' We need tools built for us."

Key Insights

  • Status reporting is duplicative - same content reformatted for different stakeholders
  • Project health indicators are manual, not automated based on actual data
  • Resource requests take days/weeks - started requesting early as a buffer
  • Time entry is stale due to poor mobile experience and team member compliance
  • Expert search in KnowledgeHub is a starting point but doesn't provide quality signals
  • Wants automated health alerts based on budget burn, milestone tracking
  • ProjectPulse designed for finance/HR, not project managers - workflow mismatch
  • Status update that should take 15 minutes takes an hour
  • Would benefit from auto-generated reports with customization options
  • Competitive comparison suggests firms are all struggling, but some improving
