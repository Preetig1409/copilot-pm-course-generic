Testing Strategy: Copilot PM Course

Version: 2.0 Date: 2026-02-17

A structured approach to course validation using 15 volunteer Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches organized into 4 teams, applying 10 testing lenses across all 15 course modules over a 2-week sprint.

Guiding Principles

PrincipleWhy it matters
Every team, every moduleAll 4 teams test all 15 modules — different eyes catch different things
Focus more, not onlyEach team applies all 10 lenses but goes deeper on their 2 owned lenses
Test like a learner, think like a coachExperience the course as the target PM, then evaluate with your practitioner expertise
Quality over volume10 validated, actionable findings beat 50 vague complaints
Two-gate validationNo feedback reaches the course author without review by own lead + a second team's lead
