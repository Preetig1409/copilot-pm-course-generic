## SLIDE 1: Opening — Purpose & What We Built

### Microsoft Copilot for Product Managers
**A 6-hour self-paced course to 10x enterprise PM productivity**

---

**The Problem We're Solving**

Product Managers spend 60%+ of their week on tasks AI can accelerate — synthesizing interviews, drafting PRDs, analyzing data, writing stakeholder communications. Most Copilot training is generic. Ours is built for how PMs actually work.

**What We Built**

A hands-on, enterprise-grade course with:
- **15 modules** across 3 progressive levels (Foundation → Advanced → Visuals)
- **Immersive Accenture context** — learners work as a Senior Product Owner on real internal products (AKX & ADH), with realistic personas, data, and governance scenarios
- **Interactive web application** with progress tracking, a prompt library, and sample files
- **14+ hours/week** of potential time savings per PM — from meeting notes (30 min → 2 min) to PRD drafts (4 hrs → 30 min)

**What You'll See Today**

1. Live walkthrough of the course web app — navigation, modules, and resources
2. Microsoft Copilot in action — a prompt demo showing real PM workflows
3. How this course enables your teams
