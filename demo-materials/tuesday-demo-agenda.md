### 3. Live Demo — Copilot in 3 Minutes (8 min)
**Key message:** *See it work, right now, on a real PM task.*

Pick ONE of these quick demos (whichever feels most relatable to the audience):

**Option A: Meeting Notes → Action Items (2 min)**
- Open Copilot Chat
- Paste raw meeting notes from `sample-files/meeting-notes/meeting-notes-raw.md`
- Prompt: "Extract decisions, action items with owners, and open questions. Format as a table."
- Show the result — what took 30+ minutes now takes seconds

**Option B: Multi-Audience Communication (2 min)**
- Give Copilot a product update
- Ask it to rewrite for 3 audiences: engineering, executive, end users
- Show how tone, detail level, and framing shift automatically

**Option C: User Interview Synthesis (3 min)**
- Upload 2-3 user interviews

After the demo: "That was one prompt. The course teaches you 50+ of these across every part of your PM workflow."

**Key message:** *Three levels, from basics to advanced — go at your own pace.*

**Level 2: Advanced PM Workflows (3 modules, ~1.5 hours)**

**Plus: Reference materials**

- Start anytime, go as fast or slow as you need

## Facilitator Notes

**Tone:** Energetic but not salesy. This is peer-to-peer, not top-down. You're showing them something that will genuinely help their daily work.

**Common questions to prepare for:**
- "Do I need technical skills?" → No. Plain English only.
- "How is this different from the generic Copilot training?" → It's built for PMs, with PM tasks, PM context, PM outputs.
- "What if I'm already using Copilot?" → Level 2 and 3 will push you further. The course goes deep on PRDs, data analysis, and strategy.
- "Is this mandatory?" → [Your call — but frame it as an opportunity, not an obligation]
- "How long will it really take?" → ~6 hours total, but you can do one 15-min module at a time.

**What to avoid:**
- Don't oversell the time savings — "5-8 hours" is the validated range, not "it will change your life"
- Don't make it sound like classroom training they have to sit through
- Don't skip the live demo — seeing is believing
