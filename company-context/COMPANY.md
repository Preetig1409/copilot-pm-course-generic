## Internal Challenges

### Current Pain Points

**1. Knowledge Fragmentation**
- 5,000+ employees generating content daily
- Past work scattered across Google Drive, Notion, Slack, email
- Teams "reinvent the wheel" - recreating proposals, analyses, runbooks
- Estimated $15M+ annual waste in duplicated effort

**2. Expert Discovery**
- Hard to find subject matter experts across departments
- Directors spend hours identifying who has relevant experience
- New hires can't navigate the organization effectively
- Customer opportunities lost due to slow expert mobilization

**3. Capacity Visibility**
- Program managers lack real-time project health data
- Available team members not allocated efficiently
- Resource allocation is manual, spreadsheet-based
- Forecasting relies on stale data

**4. Adoption of Internal Tools**
- Multiple competing platforms (legacy systems, shadow IT)
- Low adoption despite significant investment
- Employees prefer familiar consumer tools (Google, Notion)
- Training doesn't translate to daily usage
