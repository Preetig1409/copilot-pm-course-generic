Meta-Team (leads)Above + 2-3 hrs (two syncs + consolidation session)

Kickoff Agenda (Day 1, 60 minutes)

  1. Course overview — What this course is, who it's for, what success looks like (10 min)
  2. Strategy walkthrough — 10 lenses, "focus more not only" model, quality gate (15 min)
  3. Lens deep-dive — Each team reviews their 2 owned lenses, asks clarifying questions (10 min)
  4. Template walkthrough — Walk through one example finding together, submit a practice item (15 min)
  5. Team breakout — Leads confirmed, logistics set (channel, folder, schedule) (10 min)

Definition of Done

The testing cycle is complete when:

  • All 4 teams have completed all 15 modules
  • Each module has feedback from at least 3 of the 4 teams
  • All 10 lenses have been applied across the course (deep by owning team, light by others)
  • All submitted feedback has passed both checkpoints (team lead + cross-team lead)
  • Cross-team duplicates have been identified and merged by Meta-Team
  • Meta-Team has delivered the consolidated report
  • Course author has received and acknowledged the report
